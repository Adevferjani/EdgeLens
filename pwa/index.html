      ABOUT

      What it is

      EdgeLens was built for the course "Project: Edge AI" at IU International University of Applied Sciences. A face detector (YuNet) finds faces and five landmarks; two compact neural networks (MobileNetV3-Small) estimate age and gender, and facial expression. All three run in the browser with ONNX Runtime Web.

      Privacy

      Camera frames and photos are processed on this device and never sent anywhere. EdgeLens does not recognise who a person is.

      How accurate

      On the test sets, the age is off by about 5 years on average, gender is 94 % correct and expression 68 % (7 classes; happy vs. sad: 91 %). It often underestimates people over 60 and confuses sad with neutral or fear more than other expressions. Results are based on appearance only and can be wrong, especially for groups that are under-represented in the training data, so they should not be used to make decisions about people.
